Firework Shy

Same paced lessons as the Gunshy Fix. Fireworks sounds instead of gunfire. Built for dogs that shut down on the Fourth — and for hunting dogs that got a bad night of fireworks and started acting gun shy.

Ask about Firework Shy Can fireworks make a hunting dog gun shy?

Tyce’s point is simple: if the dog’s demeanor changes around the sound — even if it just sits still by you and does not run — that can still be fear. Fireworks are one of the common ways a young dog gets that first bad association.

Checkout for this track is being wired with the new site. Email or call and we will get you the current Firework Shy files.
