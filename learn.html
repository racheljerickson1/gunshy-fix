Gunshy Fix

Can a gun-shy dog be fixed?

Honest answer. No guarantee. What actually gives a dog a chance.

How to fix a gun-shy dog

Stop making it worse. Work the sound at home. Then break the wall in the field.

How long does it take to fix a gun-shy dog?

Watch the dog, not the calendar. A month of consistent work, or several months.

When does a gun-shy dog need professional training?

Birds, property, a second handler on the gun — and when home work is not enough.

My dog is scared of gunshots — what should I do?

Stop shooting around the dog. Then figure out what happened and rebuild confidence.

How to introduce a puppy to gunfire

Do not test them with a shot. Start positive. Start far away.

Signs your dog is becoming gun shy

It is a change in demeanor — not only a dog that runs to the truck.

What to do if you shot too close to your puppy

That “test” is often how the problem starts. Here is the next step.

How far away should you start gunfire with a puppy?

There is no single number. Far enough that the shot is barely noticeable.
