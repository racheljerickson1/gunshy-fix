Gunshy Fix (“we”) operates gunshyfix.com. This page explains, in plain language, the information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

If you request the free guide or contact us, we collect the email address and any message you send. If you buy the program, checkout runs on store.gunshyfix.com (Bandzoogle), which processes payment and order details separately.

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand site traffic. If you arrive from an ad, the URL may include a Google click ID (gclid) or UTM parameters so we can tell which campaign sent you.

How we use it

We use your email to send the guide you asked for, order-related messages, and Gunshy Fix training tips if you opted in. You can unsubscribe from marketing email at any time.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data with the tools that make the site work (email delivery, analytics, and the store checkout provider).
